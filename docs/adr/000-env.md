## Decision

- Toolchain is good for T03/T04/T04b (Swift 6.4, SDK 27.0 has Core Audio process taps).
- Swap rule relaxed by the mini PC parent (old swap files; stop only on critical pressure or <3 GB free disk).
- No codesigning identity: fine for `swift run` CLIs; the app bundle later needs an Apple ID in Xcode.
